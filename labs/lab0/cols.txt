Now before you actually proceed, sanity check me on these strategic moves:

(1) We never store fields that are easily computable from other fields.
Therefore, never PTS, FG_PCT, FT_PCT, FG3_PCT, REB, or WL from a game (since
that's derivable from PLUS_MINUS). That makes sense, right?

(2) We treat player's names as unique, since that seems convenient and almost
always true.

(3) we store team IDs since the same franchise changes names through history.

each player who played in it. Hence, no GAME_DATE or MATCHUP in PlayerGameLogs:
we can simply join on Game_ID to the LeagueGameFinder table.

(and her age for any game, no matter what season it was) without needing to
store Age explicitly. I feel like we still need to store Exp, though -- wdyt?

information by not storing Exp. And if we do store Exp in the CommonTeamRoster
table, we ought to be able to reconstruct "a player's years-of-experience at
the time she played in game X, no matter how long ago it was" using similar
arithmetic as for age, right?
